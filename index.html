The Story of the Castle of Dread

Centuries ago, over the lush, flourishing Kingdom of Aethelgard, dark clouds gathered. In the heart of the kingdom, within a volcano long believed dormant, the Dragon King Xykon awoke and raised the Castle of Dread. With the kingdom in ruin, Princess Lysandra disappeared. Some say she is prisoner, others claim she is the doom herself.
You, a wandering knight of uncertain fate, step onto the Lava Bridge – the only way into the cursed castle. Once you cross, there is no turning back. Glory, redemption, or death await.

Who controls the dragon? What is the fate of the princess? What secrets lie deep within these walls?
The Castle of Dread awaits.
